File: Supplementary_Table_S4_GO_BP_enrichment.txt.gz
- GO biological process enrichment of the 13 gene modules of Figure 5 (MSA or PD versus HC, lineage 1 and 3)
- one row for each GO BP gene set that has at least one gene in common with the module, also the not significant ones
- module_label: name of the module in the figures (C1, C2, ...), module_id: name used in the analysis
- msigdb_gene_set_id: MSigDB identifier of the gene set (not the GO accession), msigdb_gene_set_name: name in MSigDB, gene_set_label: readable version of the name
- module_genes_total: all genes of the module, module_genes_in_universe: genes of the module in the gene universe, background_genes_in_universe: size of the gene universe
- overlap_genes: gene symbols separated by semicolon
- p_value: enrichment p-value, fdr_bh_within_module: Benjamini-Hochberg adjusted within each module over all returned gene sets
- gene universe: genes with a valid condition test for the disease and lineage that are part of at least one GO BP gene set
- clusterProfiler enricher with MSigDB 2026.1 GO BP, gene sets with 15 to 500 genes, no p-value or q-value cutoff
