evaluateK: AIC values of tradeSeq models with 3 to 10 knots (100 genes, one file per lineage), used to choose the number of knots for Figure 4 and 5.
evaluateK takes 10 to 15 minutes per lineage with 8 cores, so Figure_4/code/05_evaluateK_Analysis.R reads these values; the call that made them is kept there as a comment.
