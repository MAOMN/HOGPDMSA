Raw data are not part of this repository. The scripts in DataPreparation read them from one folder (rawDataPath at the top of the scripts) with the following subfolders:

Folder: HOG_RNAseq
- Paired-end RNA-seq reads of the six HOG samples (three controls, three samples treated with alpha-synuclein pre-formed fibrils), twelve files:
  <sample>_S<number>_R1_001.fastq.gz and <sample>_S<number>_R2_001.fastq.gz (Ctrl1_S1, Ctrl2_S2, Ctrl3_S3, asyn1_S4, asyn2_S5, asyn3_S6)
- GEO series: GSE349624 (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE349624)

Folder: GENCODE_v47
- gencode.v47.transcripts.fa.gz, GRCh38.primary_assembly.genome.fa.gz and gencode.v47.annotation.gtf.gz
- GENCODE release 47: https://ftp.ebi.ac.uk/pub/databases/gencode/Gencode_human/release_47/

Folder: snRNAseq_HC_MSA_PD
- adata.h5ad: snRNA-seq data set of HC, MSA and PD donors (raw counts, cell annotation and UMAP of the authors), Rydbirk et al., Nature Communications 17, 5234 (2026)
  Broad Single Cell Portal, accession SCP3252 (offered there as "adata (1).h5ad", rename it to adata.h5ad)
- metadata.tsv: donor table of the same study (diagnosis, sex, age, post-mortem interval, brain bank, RNA extraction batch, flowcell), part of the data archive of the authors
  that is linked in their code repository https://github.com/rrydbirk/MSAvsPD ("Remaining data", https://osf.io/bgrqa)

Folder: ExternalPDCohorts
- Three published snRNA-seq data sets of PD and control donors, downloaded from GEO (supplementary files of the series or of the samples):
- Martirosyan_GSE243639: GSE243639_Filtered_count_table.csv.gz (counts of all cells), GSE243639_UMAP_coordinates.xlsx (cell types of the authors), GSE243639_Clinical_data.csv.gz (donor table)
  GEO series GSE243639, "Unravelling cell type specific response to Parkinson's Disease at single cell resolution" (Martirosyan et al., PubMed 38245794)
- Smajic_GSE157783: GSE157783_IPDCO_hg_midbrain_UMI.tar.gz (counts of all cells), GSE157783_IPDCO_hg_midbrain_cell.tar.gz (cell types of the authors), GSE157783_IPDCO_hg_midbrain_genes.tar.gz (genes),
  GSE157783_family.soft.gz (series file with age and sex of the donors)
  GEO series GSE157783, "Single cell atlas of the human midbrain reveals cell state specific to Parkinson's disease" (Smajic et al.)
- Lee_GSE148434: <GSM>_<donor>_snRNA.molecule_info.h5 of the twelve snRNA-seq samples GSM6459689 to GSM6459700 (molecule files of Cell Ranger, there are no count matrices or cell annotations in GEO)
  GEO series GSE148434, "Characterization of altered molecular mechanisms in Parkinson's disease through cell type-resolved multi-omics analyses" (Lee et al., PubMed 37058563)
  abo2467_Table_S1.xlsx (Table S1 of the supplementary material of the article, Lee et al., Sci Adv 9, eabo2467 (2023), DOI 10.1126/sciadv.abo2467; age and sex of the donors, which GEO does not give; in the folder of the molecule files)
